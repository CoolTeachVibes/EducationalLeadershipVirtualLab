High-Performance Team Administration: Leadership Diagnostic Simulation

Welcome to the Virtual Leadership Lab! In this practice lab, you will act as a team management supervisor. You will examine 10 real team scenarios, identify the correct leadership style, choose the right reason for your choice, and write a practical strategy to help the team improve.

Laboratory Steps & Instructions

  • Read carefully: Look at the leadership behavior and team results in each case.
  • Choose the Leadership Style: Match the situation to 1 of the 5 main leadership styles.
  • Select the Reason: Pick the option that correctly explains the leader's behavior.
  • Action Strategy: Write a thorough management plan to keep performance and teamwork high (minimum 40 words).
  • Download Your Results: At the end, you can export and save your official signed diagnostic report.
